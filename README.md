THE WORLD — 25 November 2026: a one-image newspaper summary of the day's world news, drawn deterministically with Python + Pillow. Lead story: Houthi missiles hit Riyadh and Iran claims two US drones downed over the Strait of Hormuz. Also inside: US tariffs planned for 14 major economies, Shenzhou-24 lifting off with Pakistan's first astronaut to the Tiangong station, Huawei found guilty in a US court, Poland's no-fly zone near Ukraine, and the Cairo fuel-tanker fire that killed at least 30 people — plus five more stories in the "Also Today" strip. Every story carries its own drawn visual and a caption with names and numbers, so the image explains the news on its own, with no news feed needed.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.png` (1024×1024 PNG, under 1 MiB). No network, no randomness — two runs produce byte-identical output.
